Cookie Management

Last Updated: June 2026

When visiting the Cédiapsy website, information about your terminal's navigation may be stored in files called "Cookies". This page helps you understand what a cookie is, its purpose, and how to configure it.

1. What is a "Cookie"?

A cookie is a small text file deposited on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when visiting a website. It is transmitted by the site's server to your browser and allows the sender to identify the device in which it is stored, during the cookie's validity period.

2. Cookies used on our site

The Cédiapsy website uses cookies for specific purposes, mainly focused on technical functionality and anonymous analysis:

  • Technical cookies (Strictly necessary) : They are essential for navigating the site (such as choosing your consent preferences). Without them, the site would not function correctly. They do not require your prior consent.
  • Audience measurement cookies (Anonymous statistics) : They allow us to understand how visitors arrive at our site and which pages are most read (for example, via privacy-respecting tools). These data are fully anonymized and help us improve the user experience and the clarity of our health information.

3. Third-party cookies and social networks

Some site features (such as displaying an interactive Google Maps card to locate the office at 14, rue de la Pépinière) rely on services provided by third parties. If you give your consent, these third parties may deposit cookies to track your navigation.

4. How to express or modify your choices?

In accordance with the guidelines of the CNIL and the RGPD, you are free to accept or refuse the cookies requiring consent at any time:

  • Via our cookie banner : During your first visit, a banner appears at the bottom of the screen offering you to "Accept all", "Reject all" or "Customize".
  • Via your browser settings : You can configure your browsing software so that cookies are recorded in your terminal or, conversely, that they are rejected (systematically or according to the issuer).
