Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

The present Privacy Policy has the purpose of informing users of the Cédiapsy website about the way their personal data is collected, processed and protected in strict compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act.

1. Collection of Personal Data

We collect information when you use the contact form on the website. The collected data includes mandatory or optional information:

  • Name and First Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Message Content (please refrain from transmitting highly sensitive health data in this free text)

2. Use and Purpose of the Data

All information we collect from you is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries or appointment requests.
  • To ensure the administrative follow-up of your requests prior to a potential consultation.

These data are never used for commercial, advertising or prospecting purposes.

3. Medical Confidentiality and Sharing

Your personal data is strictly confidential. As a medical and psychological practice, the information transmitted is covered by the professional secret.

We are the sole owners of the information collected on this site. Your personal information will not be sold, exchanged, transferred, or given to another company for any reason, without your consent.

4. Duration of Data Conservation

Personal data transmitted via the contact form are stored for the duration necessary for the processing of your request (generally up to 12 months if no medical follow-up is provided). If you become a patient of the Cédiapsy center, this data will be integrated into your medical file and stored according to the legal durations required by the Public Health Code.

5. Protection and Security of Information

We implement a variety of security measures to protect the security of your personal information. We use state-of-the-art encryption (HTTPS protocol / SSL certificate) to protect sensitive information transmitted online.

6. Your Rights (Access, Rectification and Deletion)

In accordance with the RGPD regulation, you have the right of access, rectification, portability and deletion of your personal data.

To exercise these rights or for any questions regarding the processing of your data, you can contact the data controller at the following address: 14, rue de la Pépinière, 75008 PARIS or by the contact email address of the practice.
