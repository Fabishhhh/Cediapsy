How does booking an appointment work?

1. Choose your practitioner

Browse our list of professionals and select the one that suits you. You can view their specialties and detailed profiles for an informed choice.

2. Select a time slot on Doctolib

Click on the booking button to be redirected to our secure Doctolib agenda. Choose the date and time that suits you among the available slots.

3. Receive your confirmation

Once your slot is validated on Doctolib, you will receive a confirmation email and SMS containing all the details of your appointment.

4. First consultation in person or via video

On the day of your appointment, please present yourself at the selected clinic or connect to the video conference via the provided link. Your practitioner will welcome you.
